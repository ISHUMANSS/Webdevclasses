Overview:
this is a simple test of what the start of a booking and admin system could look like for CabsOnline. "It allows passengers to book taxi services from any of their internet
connected computers or mobile phones". It also allows an admin team to search for specific booking or get a list of unassigned booking in the next 2 hours and then assign thm.

How to use:
navigate to booking.html 
fill in a pick up by filling in all the needed infromation and then pressing the book taxi button (make sure the time is around an hour later for best results)
take note of the booking number returned to you as you can you this to search your booking
navigate to admin.html
press search booking
    when the booking refrence is empty it will return a list of booking which are due in the next 2 hours and are NOT assigned
    when a valid booking refrence is put in that booking will be returned even if it has allready been assigned
click the assign button on the booking that shows up
booking will be assigned
